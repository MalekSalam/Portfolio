# 2022 – 2023
This project started when I noticed how many plastic bottles were thrown away at my school every day. The goal was to design a machine that could recycle these bottles and turn them into 3D-printing filament.
Using Fusion 360, I designed the full mechanical system, focusing on reliability and consistent output. I faced many challenges such as thinner bottles due to budget cuts by water suppliers, which affected how the material could be processed. Adjustments to pultrusion speed and filament thickness helped maintain consistent results.
The project taught me a lot about problem-solving, testing, and refining designs while working on a hands-on engineering challenge.
